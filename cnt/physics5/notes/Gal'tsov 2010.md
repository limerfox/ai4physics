# Gal'tsov 2010

[источник] Gal'tsov D. V., Spirin P. A. Radiation reaction in curved even-dimensional spacetime. arXiv preprint, 2010.
[источник] Аннотация: We develop a new method of computing radiation reaction for a point particle interacting with massless scalar and vector fields in curved space-time. It is based on the analysis of field Green's functions with both points lying on the particle world-line and does not require integration of the field stresses outside the world line as was used in the DeWitt-Brehme approach, thus leading to a substantial simplification of the problem.
[порождено] Зачем проекту: Новый метод вычисления радиационного трения для скалярного поля в чётномерном искривлённом пространстве-времени.
